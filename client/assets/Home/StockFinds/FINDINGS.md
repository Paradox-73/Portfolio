# Home asset library — stock pixel-art finds

Goal: extra pixel-art / animated props for the front-facing cozy bedroom on `index.html`.

## TL;DR on vibe match
Your room is **large, detailed, front-facing (flat elevation)** pixel art — it looks
custom/AI-generated and very cohesive. Almost every free pack out there is **small
game-tile art** drawn **top-down or isometric**, so nothing drops in seamlessly.
The closest in *palette* is ToffeeCraft (warm browns), but it's isometric.

For a seamless match, generating new props in your exact style (front view, same
scale/palette) is the better route — see "Recommended next step" at the bottom.

## What's in this folder
- `_previews/` — preview screenshots of the best-matching packs, named by pack, so
  you can eyeball the actual contents WITHOUT downloading each pack. A few are `_ANIM*.gif`
  (animated previews). These are only for browsing — not final assets.
- `ishtar-cozy-pack_CC0_TOPDOWN-FARM_probably-not-a-match.zip` — a real CC0 pack I
  pulled, but it turned out to be a top-down OUTDOOR farm tileset. Kept only so you can
  see what "CC0 + directly downloadable" typically looks like (usually the wrong vibe).

## Curated sources (best → okay), with licenses
Note: itch.io packs can't be auto-downloaded (download/email wall) — grab them with a
click from the links below. Licenses are as of the last check; re-read on the page.

1. **ToffeeCraft — Cozy Room (isometric)** — closest palette match (warm cozy browns).
   Isometric though. Name-your-price; free version = personal use, editing OK,
   **no redistribute/resell**. https://toffeecraft.itch.io/cozy-room-pixel-asset-pack
2. **shubibubi — Cozy Interior** — big, cute, lots of animated bits (sage-green palette).
   Free version = non-commercial only, no redistribution; commercial = paid ($3.99+).
   https://shubibubi.itch.io/cozy-interior
3. **Penzilla — Top-Down Retro Interior** — top-down, nice animated screens/props.
   Free for portfolio/non-commercial **with credit** "Graphics created by Penzilla Design";
   commercial requires purchase; no redistribution. https://penzilla.itch.io/top-down-retro-interior
4. **sierrassets — Pixel Art Furniture Pack** — 520+ furniture sprites.
   https://sierrassets.itch.io/pixel-art-furniture-pack
5. **OpenGameArt — CC0/public-domain pixel art** (fully free, safe, but mostly top-down):
   - CC0 Cozy Asset Pack: https://opengameart.org/content/cozy-asset-pack-10
   - Plant pixel art (CC0): https://opengameart.org/content/plant-pixel-art-cco
   - CC0/OGA-BY pixel art: https://opengameart.org/content/cc0oga-by-pixel-art
6. **Pixabay** — royalty-free pixel/animated gifs, no attribution (quality/relevance varies):
   https://pixabay.com/gifs/search/pixel%20art%20animation/

## Licensing note for a public portfolio
A personal portfolio is generally treated as **non-commercial**, so the free itch tiers
above are usable **with credit** and **without re-hosting the raw packs**. If you ever
monetize the site, the paid/commercial tiers or CC0-only assets are the safe choice.
